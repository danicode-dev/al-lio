# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in AL-LIO a harassment-free and
welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation or socioeconomic
status.

## Expected behaviour

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- using welcoming and inclusive language;
- respecting different viewpoints and experience levels;
- giving specific, constructive technical feedback;
- accepting responsibility and correcting mistakes;
- prioritising student safety and community benefit.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats,
sexualised content, deliberate disclosure of private information, sustained
disruption or retaliation against a reporter.

## Scope

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
project communication and public representation of the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer. Do not include
personal or sensitive information in a public issue. The maintainer may edit or
remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation
when behaviour is harmful or unsafe.

Reports will be considered with attention to the privacy and safety of everyone
involved. Bad-faith reports and retaliation are also violations.

## Attribution

This policy is based on the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html),
version 2.1.
